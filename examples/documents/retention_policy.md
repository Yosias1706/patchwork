# Retention policy

Customer-support conversations are retained for 24 months from the date of creation. The legal team may place a preservation hold on a conversation; material under a hold is not deleted until the hold is released.

At the end of the retention period, the nightly deletion job permanently removes conversation content and attachments. Aggregated, de-identified analytics may be retained after the underlying conversations are deleted.

Retention applies to conversations from email, chat, and the customer portal. Account owners may request an export of their active conversation history through support. Exports are prepared only for authorized contacts and do not override an existing preservation hold.

The privacy operations team reviews the deletion job each month. If a deletion fails, the affected records are retried and the failure is investigated. Customers with questions about this policy should contact the privacy team through their account representative.
